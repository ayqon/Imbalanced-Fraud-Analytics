Research Methodology and Design Decisions

1. Inductive Bias and Tabular Trees

Tree ensemble models (XGBoost, Random Forest) systematically outperformed Deep Neural Networks (ANN, LSTM). Because the 28 principal component features are already decorrelated, deep hierarchical representations offer little advantage over axis-aligned decision tree splits.

2. Data Leakage Prevention

StandardScaler and SMOTE oversampling were strictly fitted on the training partition. Classification decision thresholds were calibrated exclusively on the validation fold prior to evaluation on the holdout test set.

3. Unsupervised Autoencoder Anomaly Detection

Trained exclusively on legitimate transactions, the Deep Autoencoder provides zero-day detection capability by quantifying reconstruction mean-squared-error (MSE) on unseen attack patterns without relying on historical labels.

4. Regulatory Compliance (GDPR Article 22)

Under European GDPR Article 22 regulations, automated credit and transaction declines require individualized explanations. Using TreeSHAP, the system generates exact per-transaction feature attributions in sub-millisecond latency.
